## Overview

Dice & Delish is a cooking overhaul for NeoForge 1.21. It adds a small cooking loop on top of vanilla farming: grow your own produce, cook on a Grill Table and a Skillet, dice ingredients on a Cutting Board, and fill a reusable Iron Cup. Everything is data-driven, so recipes and loot are easy to extend.

## What it does

- Grow strawberries, tomatoes, lettuce, purple onions and rice, or harvest the wild crops that generate in the world.
- Cook on the Grill Table (regular or Soul) and the Skillet. The grill also takes campfire recipes.
- Dice and slice on the Cutting Board with knives from stone to netherite.
- Fill the reusable Iron Cup with milk, yogurt, strawberry yogurt or raw egg liquid.
- Optional JEI and Jade integrations show the recipes and what is cooking.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.21 to 1.21.3 with NeoForge 21 or newer, and Java 21.
- Install it on the client and the server for multiplayer.
- Optional: [JEI](https://modrinth.com/mod/jei) and [Jade](https://modrinth.com/mod/jade).

> **Coming from NerdSoft Kitchen (before 1.0.0)?** The mod ID changed, so back up your world first. Blocks and items under the old `nerdsoftkitchen` ID do not carry over.
